### Clustering metrics:

| Name                                                    | Type                                                                 | Boundary   | Optimum               | Description                                                                                           |
|---------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| [Davies-Bouldin](clustering/dbi.md)                     | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-blue)          | –          | The lower the better   | Internal metric based on a similarity notion between clusters.                                         |
| [DBCV](clustering/dbcv.md)                              | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-blue)          | [-1, 1]    | 1                      | Internal metric for evaluating density-based clustering methods.                                       |
| [Dunn Index](clustering/dunn.md)                        | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-blue)          | –          | The higher the better  | Trade-off between inter- and intra-cluster distances.                                                  |
| [Silhouette](clustering/silhouette.md)                  | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-blue)          | [-1, 1]    | 1                      | Trade-off between average inter- and intra-cluster distances.                                          |
| [Kolmogorov–Smirnov](clustering/kolmogorov_smirnov.md)  | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/External-purple)        | [0, 1]     | 1                      | Maximum difference between two empirical CDFs; close to 1 indicates highly dissimilar distributions.   |
| [Calinski–Harabasz](clustering/calinski_harabasz.md)   | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-blue)          | [0, ∞)     | The higher the better  | Ratio of between- to within-cluster dispersion; higher values indicate well-separated dense clusters. |
