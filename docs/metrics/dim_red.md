## Dimensionality reduction metrics:

 | Metric                                                    | Type                                                                                          | Description                                                                                                                    | Boundary     | Optimum               |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------|------------------------|
| [Adjusted Jaccard Distance (AJD)](dimred/ajd.md)                 | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Quantifies how much local neighborhood structure is preserved after dimensionality reduction.                                | [0, 1]       | 0                      |
| [co-KNN (AUC & size)](dimred/co_knn.md)                          | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Evaluates how well local neighborhood structures are preserved.                                                               | –            | The higher the better  |
| [Continuity](dimred/trust_continuity.md)                         | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Measures how many high-dim neighbors are preserved in low dimensions.                                                        | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
| [Distance correlation](dimred/distance_correlation.md)           | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Global-darkgreen)                              | Detects linear and nonlinear dependencies; equals 0 only when variables are independent.                                      | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
| [Density Preservation](dimred/density_preservation.md)           | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Measures how well local point densities are maintained after dimensionality reduction.                                       | [-1, 1]      | 1                      |
| [Entourage](dimred/entourage.md)                                 | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Quantifies local neighborhood preservation quality (structural fidelity).                                                     | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
| [Graph Edit Distance (GED)](dimred/ged.md)                       | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Global-darkgreen)                              | Quantifies graph dissimilarity via minimum edits between two neighborhood graphs.                                            | –            | The lower the better   |
| [Kruskal's Stress](dimred/kruskal_stress.md)                     | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Global-darkgreen)                              | Based on the difference between distances in high and low dimensions.                                                         | [0, 1]       | 0                      |
| [LCMC](dimred/lcmc.md)                                           | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Evaluates changes in the nearest neighbor matrix after dimension reduction.                                                  | –            | The higher the better  |
| [Local & Global co-KNN](dimred/local_global_coknn.md)            | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local%20%2B%20Global-green)                    | Measures preservation of both local and global neighborhood structure.                                                       | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
| [Spearman's Rho](dimred/spearman_rho.md)                         | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Global-darkgreen)                              | Based on the difference in rank-ordering of distances between high and low dimensions.                                        | [0, ∞)       | 0                      |
| [Trustworthiness](dimred/trust_continuity.md)                    | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Measures how well low-dimensional neighbors match those in the high-dimensional space.                                        | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
| [Trustworthiness and Continuity](dimred/trust_continuity.md)     | ![Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/DimRed%20Local-yellowgreen)                             | Combines trustworthiness and continuity scores for neighborhood structure conservation.                                      | [0, 1]       | 1                      |
